Sources
A chronological RSS reader pulling from ~63 sources across wire services, broadcasters, investigative outlets, policy journals, and local press. No ads, no algorithms, no affiliations.
Switch to Images via the tab bar to browse a photo grid from the same sources. Tap an image to reveal the caption and article link. Adjust column density (1 / 3 / 5 / 9) using the Grid control in the header.
To install: on iOS, open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open in Chrome → menu → Add to Home Screen.
